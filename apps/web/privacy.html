Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026

Gantry is a demo built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. This page says, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit.

The short version

  • No accounts and no emails. We never ask who you are.
  • Visits are counted without cookies.
  • Your session is wiped after your visit, with everything you did in it.
  • What you say to Remy goes to the AI companies that run her, listed below.

Counting visits

Vercel Web Analytics counts page views, and Vercel Speed Insights measures how fast the pages load. Neither uses cookies. They record the page, where the visit came from, the country, and the kind of browser and device, never who you are. We send them the page’s address only, without anything after it, so no link or code to your session is ever recorded. Vercel explains what it collects in its Web Analytics privacy notes.

Your session

When you start a session, you get your own copy of the Harbor Street Offices plan. It’s kept in our database, run by Supabase, while you use it. Only the people you invite, with the link or the session code, see it. After your visit it’s wiped, with the chat and everything you changed.

You pick a name from our list; you never type one.

Your browser keeps what the app needs to bring you back to your session (a teammate tab keeps its own until you close it), and a few display settings, until you clear it. None of it is used to follow you anywhere else.

When a session code someone types matches no session, we note the time against their visit for 10 minutes, to stop guessing.

Talking to Remy

  • In the chat: the messages you send Remy go to Anthropic’s Claude, which writes her answers.
  • On a call: your voice goes to OpenAI, which makes out what you said. ElevenLabs turns Remy’s written answers into her voice; it gets only her text, never yours. LiveKit carries the audio between everyone on the call. We don’t record or keep the audio.

Each of these companies handles what it receives under its own policy: Anthropic, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and LiveKit. Please don’t tell Remy anything private.

Payments

Every payment runs on PayPal’s sandbox, which moves no real money. If a PayPal window opens, it’s PayPal’s sandbox: sign in only with a sandbox test account, never your own PayPal login, and never enter real card or bank details.

The film

The film plays in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. After that, YouTube’s player works under Google’s privacy policy.

Keeping the demo up

Vercel hosts the site and, like any web host, sees your IP address to send you the pages (Vercel’s privacy notice). Before a session starts, Cloudflare Turnstile may check that a person, not a script, is asking (Cloudflare’s privacy policy).
